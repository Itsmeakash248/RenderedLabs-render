## 1. Non-Negotiable Craft Law For This Film

1. **Palette:** `parchment #F5EFEB`, `vellum #EAE0D0`, `ivory #FAF7F0`, `ink #1E1813`, `sepia #5C5248`, `umber #2B1F17`, `terracotta #C2593F`, `amber #D9822B`, `brass #C89D42`. Pure `#000000` and `#FFFFFF` banned. Cosmic navy, neon cyan, electric violet banned.
2. **Thickness:** hero structural strokes **16–32px** (target 24–32), connectors **8–16px**, scene detail floor **≥5px**, no 1–2px hero wireframes. Solid or blooming gradient fills only. Pill capsules `border-radius:100px`. Beads `r=12–20px`.
3. **Progressive construction:** every scene executes 4 phases across its slot — (1) **Vector Origin and Path Growth** 0–30%, (2) **Volumetric Fill Bloom** 25–60%, (3) **Mechanical Socketing and Living Hold** 50–85%, (4) **Payoff and Seam Handoff** 80–100%. No whole-object fade, slide or sticker pop. Plate reveals are matte wipes inside a growing passe-partout, never opacity-only.
4. **Motion depth:** every scene couples X+Y drift with scale and rotation, and carries perspective tilt, isometric projection, or ≥3-plane parallax (FG / MG / BG). World backgrounds are **strictly 2D**.
5. **Living hold:** minimum micro-motion per scene — camera 1.000→1.025–1.035, paper-grain shimmer at 1.6s cadence, ember or dust motes, at least one articulated part still breathing. No frame is static for 2.0s.
6. **Text:** ≤3 words per scene, labels and badges only; text enters after the visual state is legible and holds ≥1.2s. VO carries all prose.
7. **Seams:** `swift` 0.2–0.3s per half (default), `heavy` 0.6–0.9s per half only at act breaks, mid-rolls and payoff. Every heavy seam maps to a `sub`/`impact`/`whoosh` hit in the adjacent scene. Zero repeated moves.
