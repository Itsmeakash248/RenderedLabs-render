- **Progressive construction only.** Each scene runs STYLE_GUIDE §3 phases 1–4: path growth → volumetric fill bloom → mechanical socketing → payoff/handoff. Zero pop-ins, zero fade-in cards, zero thin wireframes.

- **Light floor.** Named source per scene (practical, inspection window, floodlight, dawn, archival bounce). Ember leads payoff frames; violet carries scale/uncertainty; solar-gold is reserved for measured proof (clocks, bolts, seal, dates).
- **Text ceiling.** ≤3 words per scene, labels/badges only, zero sentences. Type: Archivo Black / Quicksand per STYLE_GUIDE §2, `core-black` outline 8–14 px at video scale.
- **Company logos:** no tech company, AI lab, model, chipmaker or cloud platform is named in the narration, so no `.media/index.md` mark is required. Institutional identity is carried by the Soviet stamp, the CIA document plate, and the exhibition banner typography — never fake corporate logos.
